A satirical driving companion for Android. It feels every bump through your phone's accelerometer and answers with a desi sound effect, played right over your music and through Android Auto.

Think of it as a live soundtrack for the Indian driving experience.

🚧 Why?

Navigation apps tell us where to go. Music apps tell us what to listen to.

But who celebrates the tiny earthquake you just drove over?

Gandkari Detector is a playful take on everyday driving on Indian roads, where every trip comes with surprise terrain, bonus speed breakers, and the odd pothole that has its own pin code.

🗺️ Coming soon: India's Bump Atlas

Right now every bump gets a sound effect. Next, every bump becomes a data point.

Put enough drives together, let AI separate real potholes from someone dropping their phone, and we get a living map of the country's speed breakers, potholes, and surprise craters: which road, which stretch, and how hard it hits.

🎵 Features

  • Bump detection: the accelerometer picks up road impacts. Hard braking and turns get filtered out (mostly).
  • Reactive sound effects: desi reaction clips. Pick a favourite or let it randomise across the ones you tick.
  • Bring your own chaos: import any clip from your phone, or search for new ones online (experimental).
  • Plays nice with your music: Spotify and friends duck for a second instead of pausing.
  • Android Auto ready: effects come through the car speakers like a navigation prompt.
  • Live bump graph: watch the road's mood swings over the last 30 seconds.
  • Tunable outrage: set sensitivity and cooldown so a rough road doesn't turn into a DJ set.
  • Private by default: no account, no location, no microphone. Bump detection never leaves your phone; anonymous usage stats help us improve and can be turned off in Settings.

📲 Install

  1. On your Android phone, tap Download for Android above.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. While parked, open Settings and pick your sensitivity and sounds.
  4. Mount the phone securely, tap Start session, and drive normally.

🛣️ Our philosophy

We believe every bump deserves to be heard.

We're not fixing the roads. We're adding a soundtrack to them. And soon, a map of exactly where to start.

⚠️ Disclaimer

This is a satire project made for entertainment. The name and the caricature are parody, and the project is not affiliated with or endorsed by any person, ministry, or government body.

Bump detection is approximate. It may trigger on other movements or miss some bumps entirely, much like the potholes themselves.

Drive safely. Keep your eyes on the road, follow traffic laws, and never touch your phone while driving. Set everything up before you leave, then let the app do its thing in the background. Keep the volume low enough to hear horns, sirens, and the vehicles around you.
