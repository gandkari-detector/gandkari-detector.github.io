Install.
Start. Drive.

Two steps. Everything else comes set up. The app is not on the Play Store, so Android may ask one extra question the first time.

Download for Android

Free. Android 8.0 or newer. About 3 MB.

Steps

  1. 1

    Install. Tap Download for Android and open the file. If Android says unknown apps are blocked, tap Settings, allow your browser, then tap Install.

  2. 2

    Start session. Mount the phone and tap Start session. Sensitivity, cooldown and sounds come tuned, so there is nothing to set. On Android Auto, clips play through the car speakers.

Before you drive

  • Start the session before you leave. Never touch the phone while driving.
  • A loose phone feels every bump twice. Use a proper mount.
  • Too chatty or too quiet? Sensitivity and sounds can be changed in Settings later, while parked.

Updating

Download the APK again from this page and install it over the old one. Your settings stay.

Drive
safely.

Eyes on the road. Follow traffic laws and never touch your phone while driving. Set everything up first, then let the app work in the background.
